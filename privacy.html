PillBell — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

The short version. PillBell keeps your medicines and reminders on your phone. There is no PillBell account and no PillBell server — your medicine list is never uploaded anywhere. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and it can send an optional problem report that contains no personal details. That is the whole of it.

Who we are

PillBell is an Android medication reminder published by Mehraz Rumman. For any question about this policy or your data, contact mehrazrumman33@icloud.com.

What stays on your phone, always

Everything you enter into PillBell is stored in a database on your own device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • Medicine names, dosages, strengths and notes
  • Your schedules, dose times and dose history
  • Stock counts and refill information
  • Caregiver or family names and relationships you type in
  • App settings and language preference

We have no server, no account system and no way to read any of this. If you uninstall PillBell, this data is deleted with it. Please back up anything you need before uninstalling.

Camera and medicine scanning

PillBell can use your camera to read text from a medicine pack. The recognition runs entirely on your device using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition.

  • Photographs are not saved to your phone's gallery or to app storage.
  • Photographs are not uploaded anywhere, to us or to Google.
  • Only the text you confirm on screen is kept, and it is kept on your device.

The camera permission is optional. Declining it disables scanning; every other part of the app continues to work, and you can always type medicine details in by hand.

Advertising

PillBell is free and is funded by occasional full-screen ads served by Google AdMob. To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes certain information, including your device's advertising ID, device and network information, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

How we have configured it:

  • Ads are requested as non-personalised — they are not based on a profile of your interests or behaviour.
  • Ad content is limited to a "G" (general audiences) rating.
  • We never send your medicines, schedules, health information or any identifier of yours to any advertiser.
  • Ads never appear on a reminder, on the alarm screen, or while you are confirming a dose.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies. See Google's Privacy & Terms for business and the list of Google advertising partners.

Your choices about ads

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or a US state with its own privacy law, PillBell shows you a consent form the first time it is able to request an ad, and you can reopen it at any time from More → Remove ads → Ad privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android under Settings → Google → Ads.
  • You can remove ads permanently with a one-time purchase from More → Remove ads.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Your payment details are processed by Google and are never seen by, or sent to, us. We receive only confirmation from Google that the purchase belongs to your Play account, which the app stores on your device so ads stay off.

Notifications and alarms

PillBell asks to post notifications and to schedule exact alarms so your reminders arrive at the right minute. These run locally on your device. No reminder, and no fact about whether you took a dose, is sent anywhere.

Children

PillBell is a general-audience app intended for adults managing their own or a family member's medication. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because your medicine data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Uninstalling PillBell removes all of it. Problem reports you have already sent remain in our bug tracker; to have one removed, contact us at the address above and tell us the report number shown in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, note it in the app's release notes.
